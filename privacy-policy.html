Privacy Policy

3D Sort Master (Android, package com.linhnv.sortmaster)
Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the 3D Sort Master game ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases and no analytics. We do not run any server that receives your data.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your device so the game can continue where you left off:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters and discovered collection items.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, reduced effects and language.
  • Ad frequency counters (for example, when the last ad was shown), used to limit how often ads appear.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to receive a reward) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are never shown while you are playing a level.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, on Google's behalf:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAd delivery, frequency capping, personalised ads (where you allow it), fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and compatibility
Ad interactions (ad shown, clicked, rewarded) and crash/performance diagnostics of the SDKAnalytics for advertising, fraud prevention

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is transmitted encrypted. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can accept or refuse personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • You can stop all data handling by the app by uninstalling it.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, as the ad provider described in section 2.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we will help you remove it.

7. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. Communication with Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold any of your data on our side, most requests are fulfilled by clearing the app's data, changing your consent in Settings, or contacting Google about data it collects. You can also contact us at the address below.
